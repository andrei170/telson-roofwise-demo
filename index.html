Questions we get asked

Listed building work, answered plainly

The questions owners and managing agents ask us most.

Do I need listed building consent for a roof repair?

Often yes, even for like-for-like. Repairs that alter the appearance or fabric of a listed building usually need consent, and doing the work without it is a criminal offence rather than a planning slap on the wrist. We will tell you what we think needs consent before we quote, and we are used to working alongside conservation officers.

Why does listed work cost more than ordinary roofing?

Because the materials and methods are different, not because of the postcode. Lime mortar rather than cement, matched brick and slate rather than the nearest stock item, and hand work where a modern job would use a machine. It is slower by design — that is what makes it reversible and what keeps the building sound.

Can you work with our managing agent?

Yes. A good part of our work comes through block management companies, where contractors are carefully vetted. We are used to written specifications, occupied buildings and residents who need to know what is happening and when.

Are you insured and accredited?

We are members of the National Federation of Roofing Contractors. Full insurance details are provided with every quotation.

Do you only do listed buildings?

No. Listed and historic work is our specialism, but a lot of what we do is ordinary — extensions, refurbishments, roof repairs, guttering and fascias for private clients across Surrey and London.
